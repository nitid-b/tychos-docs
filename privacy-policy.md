# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: April 13, 2026 · tychos.org**

Tychos is a free educational platform built by teachers, for teachers. We take student privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights that schools and students have.


## 1. Who We Are

Tychos (tychos.org) is a personal project by Steve Temple, Winston Wolff, John Burk, and other volunteers.  We are teachers and parents ourselves and this is an educational not commercial project.

For privacy questions, contact us at: **team@tychos.org**

## 2. What Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the platform:

* Account information: name and email address (student accounts are typically set up via a class join code provided by a teacher)
* Educational content: scenarios, simulations, and code written by students
* Teacher feedback and comments attached to student work
* Goal completion records and class assignments
* Standard usage and log data (browser type, IP address, pages visited)

We do not collect Social Security numbers, financial information, or sensitive personal identifiers.

## 3. How We Use Data

**Student data is used solely to provide and improve the Tychos educational service.** We do not use student data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or any commercial purpose unrelated to education.

Specifically, we use data to:

* Operate student and teacher accounts and class management
* Display student work and teacher feedback within the platform
* Track goal progress and assignments
* Improve platform performance and features

## 4. We Do Not Sell Student Data

Tychos does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose student personal information to third parties for commercial purposes. Ever.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Tychos uses the following third-party service to operate:

* **Google** — used for authentication (Google Sign-In).  When a student logins
  in through their Google account, we capture the user's name and email to
  create an automatic student account.  Google's use of data in this context is
  governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Instructure Canvas** — used for authentication (Sign-In). When a Canvas
  account connects to Tychos, we capture the user's name and email to create an
  automatic student account.
* Heap Analytics — anonymous analytics on what pages are being used.

We do not use third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

## 6. FERPA Compliance

Tychos respects the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). When schools use Tychos, the school acts as the data controller for student education records. Tychos acts as a service provider, accessing student data only as directed by and on behalf of the school.

Schools may request a Data Privacy Agreement (DPA) by contacting us at team@tychos.org.

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain student data for as long as an account is active or as needed to provide the service. Schools and teachers may request deletion of student data at any time by contacting us. Upon a verified deletion request, we will remove the relevant data within 30 days.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls that limit who can view student information. In the event of a data breach that affects student data, we will notify affected schools promptly.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top and notify schools via the email address on file. Continued use of Tychos after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
